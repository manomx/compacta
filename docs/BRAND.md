# Compacta brand assets

The mark is the existing Compacta **condensed C**: two open arcs converge on a
solid center. The arcs represent layers of conversation; the dot represents
concentrated context. The open right edge keeps the C readable at menu bar sizes.

Primary orange: `#F76B15`; highlight: `#FFB067`; deep orange: `#C9470A`.
Light background: `#FAF7F4`; ink: `#241E1A`. Keep clear space of at least one dot
diameter. Do not stretch, rotate the static mark, or close the arcs. Motion in the
app indicates real preparation; a promotional asset should not imply live status.

SVG assets use no external images or scripts. Typography uses the system font
stack. The app bundles Plus Jakarta Sans with its OFL notice. Keep an accessible
text label whenever the logo is used as an action.
